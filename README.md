# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across various product categories. The primary goal is to uncover actionable insights regarding spending patterns, customer segmentation, product preferences, and subscription behaviors to help guide strategic business decisions.

## 📊 Dataset Summary
*   **Total Rows:** 3,900
*   **Total Columns:** 18
*   **Customer Demographics:** Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status
*   **Purchase Details:** Item Purchased, Category, Purchase Amount, Season, Size, Color
*   **Shopping Behavior:** Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Shipping Type
*   **Missing Data:** 37 values in the Review Rating column were handled during the data cleaning process.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (Python)
Data preparation and cleaning were conducted using Python and `pandas`:
*   **Missing Data Handling:** Imputed missing values in the Review Rating column using the median rating of each specific product category.
*   **Standardization:** Converted column names to snake_case for consistency and readability.
*   **Feature Engineering:** Generated an `age_group` column by binning ages and a `purchase_frequency_days` column.
*   **Data Consistency:** Dropped the `promo_code_used` column after verifying it was redundant with `discount_applied`.
*   **Database Integration:** Connected the Python script directly to MySQL to load the cleaned DataFrame for SQL querying.

### 2. Data Analysis (MySQL)
Structured SQL queries were used to answer key business questions:
*   **Revenue by Gender:** Analyzed total revenue generated by male ($157,890) versus female ($75,191) customers.
*   **High-Spending Discount Users:** Identified 839 customers who utilized discounts but maintained a spend above the average purchase amount.
*   **Top Rated Products:** Identified the top 5 highest-rated items: Gloves (3.86), Sandals (3.84), Boots (3.82), Hat (3.80), and Skirt (3.78).
*   **Shipping & Spend:** Compared the average purchase amounts between Express ($60.48) and Standard ($58.46) shipping types.
*   **Subscription Value:** Compared 1,053 subscribers against 2,847 non-subscribers to evaluate average spend and total revenue. 
*   **Customer Segmentation:** Categorized customers based on purchase history into Loyal (3,116), Returning (701), and New (83) segments.
*   **Revenue by Age Group:** Calculated total revenue contributions, revealing Young Adults ($62,143) and Middle-aged ($59,197) as the top demographics.

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)
An interactive "Customer Behavior Dashboard" was developed in Power BI to present insights visually, featuring metrics like the $59.76 average purchase amount, average review ratings, and dynamic revenue breakdowns by category, age, and subscription status.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following strategic actions are recommended:
*   **Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits tailored for subscribers to increase adoption.
*   **Customer Loyalty Programs:** Reward repeat buyers to transition them into the highly profitable "Loyal" segment.
*   **Review Discount Policy:** Strategically balance sales boosts from discounts with overall profit margin control.
*   **Product Positioning:** Actively highlight top-rated and best-selling products in upcoming marketing campaigns.
*   **Targeted Marketing:** Focus marketing efforts and resources on high-revenue age groups and customers who prefer express shipping.
